# Experiment recipes

## Recipe 1: Free sovereign AI and inequality

Prompt:

> Use @nvidia-korea-econophysics-lab to test whether universal free sovereign AI reduces access inequality but leaves income polarization when capability, job mobility, premium quality, and AI-capital ownership remain unequal. Build a Korean-calibrated Python ABM, paired Monte Carlo experiments, a React/Three.js lab, and a JEIC-ready paper outline.

Required contrasts: market, free access, access plus training, transition support, citizen ownership, and integrated policy. Scan premium-quality gap × occupational mobility and report candidate phase boundaries.

## Recipe 2: Youth career-ladder displacement

Prompt:

> Use @nvidia-korea-econophysics-lab to study junior task displacement and the loss of learning-by-doing. Calibrate age, education, occupation, and region strata; compare apprenticeship guarantees, training, hiring subsidies, and access-only policy; report mobility and entry-employment effects.

Add an explicit stock of task experience and ablate the career-ladder mechanism.

## Recipe 3: Regional diffusion and homophily

Prompt:

> Use @nvidia-korea-econophysics-lab to model AI skill diffusion across Korean regions under homophilous networks. Test regional compute hubs, local mentors, remote training, and cross-region bridging ties. Produce phase diagrams over homophily × peer-learning strength.

Report network diagnostics and compare k-nearest, small-world, and randomized networks.

## Recipe 4: AI ownership versus transfers

Prompt:

> Use @nvidia-korea-econophysics-lab to compare equal-cost cash transfers, progressive AI dividends, citizen funds, and worker ownership. Track labor income, capital income, wealth concentration, welfare, and policy budget identities over 20 years.

Include policy withdrawal to test hysteresis and distinguish a flow transfer from accumulated ownership.

## Recipe 5: Economics classroom laboratory

Prompt:

> Use @nvidia-korea-econophysics-lab to create a semester project in which students formulate one falsifiable hypothesis, prepare a privacy-preserving NVIDIA Korea profile, calibrate two Korean official margins, run a 2×2 factorial ABM, and explain results in a reproducible notebook and interactive dashboard.

Grade data provenance, model transparency, falsification, uncertainty, and claim discipline rather than visual polish alone.

## Minimum deliverables for every recipe

- `research-contract.md`
- validated aggregate profile and calibration register
- versioned configuration and deterministic seeds
- tests and paired Monte Carlo results
- sensitivity, ablation, and finite-size diagnostics
- paper or report with ODD and equation-to-code crosswalk
- reproducible README
- visible synthetic-data and non-causal limitations
